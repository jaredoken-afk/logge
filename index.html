Every price is sourced — not guessed

Logge's AI handles language. Pricing comes from a deterministic rating engine, and every number it returns is traced to one of four data tiers. If a leg has no data, Logge says so.

Live rates topped up straight from carriers and storage providers.

Tier 3

Aggregator platforms

Market benchmarks to fill and sanity-check the picture.

Tier 4

AI-drafted RFQs

For legs with no API, Logge drafts RFQs to agents, truckers and warehouses.
